What’s included

Every hood cleaning covers the full path grease takes out of your kitchen.

  • Hood canopy

    Inside and outside surfaces, seams, corners and light fixtures.

  • Baffle filters

    Pulled, soaked in degreaser and pressure washed.

  • Plenum and grease troughs

    The hidden space behind the filters where buildup is heaviest.

  • Ductwork

    Cleaned through the accessible points between the hood and the roof.

  • Rooftop exhaust fan

    Blades, housing and the area around it, then a test run.

  • Cleanup

    Plastic sheeting down, kitchen wiped down and ready for your next service.

How a cleaning goes

  1. Cover the cook line

    Equipment off and cool, then wrapped in plastic to catch grease and rinse water.

  2. Pull every filter

    Filters come out to open up the plenum and get washed separately.

  3. Break down the grease

    Heavy buildup scraped, then degreaser applied to the canopy and plenum.

  4. Hot-water pressure wash

    Rinsed until the grease is gone and the steel shows through.

  5. Ductwork and rooftop fan

    Duct access points, fan blades and housing cleaned.

  6. Reassemble and check

    Filters back in, fan test-run, kitchen cleaned up.

How often does your hood need cleaning?

NFPA 96, the fire code standard for commercial kitchen exhaust systems, sets how often a system must be inspected for grease buildup based on how much and what you cook. When the inspection finds buildup, the system gets cleaned.

Your local fire marshal or insurance carrier may set a stricter schedule.

Hood cleaning questions

Do I have to close the kitchen?

The cooking equipment has to be off and cool while we work, so we schedule hood cleanings outside your service hours.

How long does a hood cleaning take?

It depends on the number of hoods and how long the duct runs are. You’ll get a time estimate along with your quote.

Do you clean the exhaust fan on the roof?

Yes. The rooftop fan collects grease from the whole system, so it’s part of every hood cleaning we do.

What do the filters need between cleanings?

Baffle filters should be washed regularly by your staff, often weekly for busy kitchens. A professional cleaning handles everything the filters can’t catch.
