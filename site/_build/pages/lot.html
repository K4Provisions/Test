What we pressure wash

Any hard surface around your building that customers drive or walk on.

  • Parking lots

    Oil and fluid stains, tire marks and built-up grime in the stalls and drive lanes.

  • Drive-thru lanes

    Grease, spilled drinks and the dark trail that builds up at the window.

  • Sidewalks and entries

    Gum, foot-traffic grime and stains at the front door.

  • Storefronts

    Walls, columns and curbs along the front of the building.

  • Dumpster areas

    The grease and runoff around the dumpster enclosure.

How we clean a lot

  1. Clear the debris

    Loose trash, leaves and grit swept up so the wash reaches the surface.

  2. Pre-treat stains

    Oil spots and grease get a degreaser to loosen them first.

  3. Pressure wash

    Surface cleaning in even passes so the lot dries clean, without stripes.

  4. Rinse and check

    Runoff rinsed away and stubborn spots treated again.

Parking lot questions

Do you need the lot empty?

The emptier the better, so most lot cleanings are scheduled when you’re closed or traffic is low. We can also work section by section if you stay open.

Will every oil stain come out?

Most fresh and moderate stains lift. Old stains that have soaked deep into concrete usually fade a lot but can leave a shadow. We’ll tell you what to expect when we see the lot.

Can you do this on the same visit as a hood cleaning?

Often, yes. Ask for both when you request a quote and we’ll price them together.
