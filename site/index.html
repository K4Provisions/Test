Step 1 of 6

Cover the cook line

With the equipment off and cool, we wrap the cooking line in plastic sheeting. Grease and rinse water get collected instead of landing on your fryers, ranges and floor.

Step 2 of 6

Pull every filter

All of the baffle filters come out so we can reach the plenum behind them, where grease builds up out of sight. The filters get soaked and washed on their own.

Step 3 of 6

Break down the grease

We scrape off the heaviest buildup first, then apply a commercial degreaser to the canopy and plenum so it can work into the layers underneath.

Step 4 of 6

Hot-water pressure wash

High-pressure hot water rinses the hood, plenum and filters until the grease is gone and the stainless steel shows through again.

Step 5 of 6

Ductwork and rooftop fan

Grease rides the airflow up the duct to the exhaust fan on your roof. We clean the duct access points, fan blades and housing so nothing is left burning up there.

Step 6 of 6

Reassemble and check

Filters go back in, the fan gets a test run, the sheeting comes down and the kitchen is wiped down for your next service. You get before-and-after photos of the job.

How often does your hood need cleaning?

NFPA 96, the fire code standard for commercial kitchen exhaust systems, sets how often a system must be inspected for grease buildup based on how much and what you cook. When the inspection finds buildup, the system gets cleaned.

Your local fire marshal or insurance carrier may set a stricter schedule.

Common questions

Do I have to close the kitchen?

The cooking equipment has to be off and cool while we work, so we schedule hood cleanings outside your service hours.

How long does a hood cleaning take?

A single-hood kitchen usually takes a few hours. Larger systems with long duct runs take longer. You’ll get a time estimate along with your quote.

Do you clean the exhaust fan on the roof?

Yes. The rooftop fan collects grease from the whole system, so it’s part of every hood cleaning we do.

What do the filters need between cleanings?

Baffle filters should be washed regularly by your staff, often weekly for busy kitchens. A professional cleaning handles everything the filters can’t catch.

Can you pressure wash the outside on the same visit?

Often, yes. Ask about cleaning your parking lot, sidewalks or dumpster pad when you request a quote and we’ll price it together.
